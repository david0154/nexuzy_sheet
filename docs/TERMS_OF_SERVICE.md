## 1. What Nexuzy Sheet is

Nexuzy Sheet is a spreadsheet workspace for Windows, macOS and Linux. It
provides an Excel-like grid, a Python-powered calculation engine with a
built-in library of spreadsheet formulas, multiple sheets, templates, charts,
formatting, import and export, and an optional **offline AI assistant**.

The AI assistant runs entirely on your computer. It uses a locally downloaded
language model and a local inference runtime; no request leaves your machine.
